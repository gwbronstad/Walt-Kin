Sep. 26-Oct 27, 1963 Letter from Anne Whaling to Walton Bronstad

Apt 837, University Terrace 
Ann Arbor, MI 48104

3320 Daniels
Dallas, TX 75205

Dear Walton,


I have been promising myself ever since your letter came that I would write something in reply that would be worthy of the occasion. But the fragments I had been working on so laboriously will not do, and now I’m suddenly faced with a major undertaking, which will take most of my time and energy for the next two weeks. I’ll spare you the details until I know how it will turn out, but briefly, there is a possibility that I may substitute for someone on a professional meeting program in December. The material I have doesn’t really fit – I’m very doubtful about it, but I’ve promised to see what I can do. So I will renounce letter writing of the kind I hope to do – the really thoughtful, carefully stated kind – until this crisis is over.


I just wanted to thank you for your letter, most generous and reassuring and done with your characteristic economy of means. I know that I do not understand it all, but as long as it is possible to write, this does not matter. Things can be made clear


(I did not know how strong this belief that the truth is possible can set us free could be until I came very near complete despair only recently. No other belief for me (even though not consciously questioned) has ever had the kind of sheltering strength.) I know that this is not strictly rational, various things social pressures primarily - can and do prevail. And given the unimaginable complexity of human behavior we can only approach more or less nearly, anything like truth. But when if anyone cares about truth much can be done and I think human beings tend to care. Their truth is own the whole attractive perhaps that is all, but that is something.


I do feel guilty (to borrow an expression,) when think of the time and effort required to write a very formal letter such as your last one I wish so much that you would consider just writing intermittently on anything within reach (as I suggested in the notes I left for you the last day.) since you write only for me, it would it could not possibly matter how they look, and I would value them even more than a formal letter.


(Later)


This is all I have been able to do after hours of effort. I had written and rewritten and then thrown it all away, as I never have before, unable to throw off the severe anxiety that made me question every word. Then, after a sleepless night, your present arrived, and I suddenly recovered a little of my courage. It was so like you – so beautifully right and appropriate and I was amazed that you must have remembered my tea in the office. I’ll take some of it to school, but keep most of it here, as I’m here much more now.


(The next day)


Again I broke off after hours of completely feudal effort (I just counted eight pages that I have destroyed, and in two evenings of uninterrupted effort, I have used up my supply of paper.) as I’ve tried to analyze this unprecedented failure, I think it’s first, my fear of troubling you in any possible way when you already under so many kinds of strain. But this simple alternative, a shallow conventional letter, which is completely false. (as almost all letters are) is also we intolerable. I faced the same problem, always in talking to you, but it seemed possible to meet it then, and I thought that I could write the same way. Perhaps the trouble is that the communication which seemed so simple and natural and so sure, was broken off before you left, after August 4 without warning and without any explanation or clue (that I have been able to find, though I have tried very hard.) it is inevitable, I suppose, that I should question everything now, but even so, if I just have time, I’m sure I can write something acceptable.


(Tuesday morning)


I have just discarded three more long and painfully elaborated paper another long evenings effort. . I will not throw these away, and perhaps one day they can be sent on, but not now. Nothing matters at the moment, but your comfort and well-being, and question that are two searchting (however important) crucial issues and anything else can wait. (Perhaps I feel driven to try to put down things that matter so much because I do not feel sure that I will be able to write again. Even though you have spoken encouragingly, the same forces – whatever they were – that made it impossible for you to talk to me after August 4 may prevent you answering now. If that should be, so, please remember that I will not lose either faith or hope, knowing that someday – if you continue to grow – you will look back at these things and see them differently.)


This is not a letter at all, but a series of statements that I am unable to write a letter. I am sending it on simply because I want to thank you for the box of tea and let you know that I will try again if you give your permission. I have promised, since I began this to try to do the paper against my better judgment, I might add, but I must try to write something. The withdrawal (personal, that is) is now as complete as I can make it and continue to hold a job at all, and I want to try to use my education in this way, since nothing else seems possible.


I hope that you will let me try again to write. I see so little of people that I have little news to offer, but there are always books. (some of the short stories in our new book, the one I sent Pat, are new to me and exciting) and ideas. And I am always eager to think about and respond to what you are thinking about, when you feel ready to tell me something about it. Whenever I reach the point of resolving that I will not trouble you by writing, I remember that it will take time for the men you work with to get to know you, and until they can understand a little, there is no one to defend you against yourself. As long as you are defenseless, my job is not quite done, and I will keep on trying with a loyalty and concern that become even deeper as I understand more and continue to grow myself. (as I hope I still do)


Please do not feel that you ever have to answer or comment on what I write – a postcard is ample acknowledgment.(I would like to know whether you read the notes I left for you that last day I thought when I found you had left them in the office there perhaps felt you could not – they were very simple and general but they were things that seemed very important, and if you did not see them, perhaps I can try again some day.) Please try to care for yourself as you care for others.


Anne


I did not get to hear Adalai Stevenson (who has long been my great admiration) because I had to send an immediate answer about the paper – the attack of course just dramatized for the outside world what I see every day


Letter's Context

2026-11-18

Anxiety and tentativity mark this letter. The mentor - mentee relationship can fray and fail, and both Anne and I saw this was happening. As senior in this duet, she would have felt the most responsibility and the greatest need to repair the wound.


This is painful to reread, Anne is under tremendous strain, and the only thing I can do to ameliorate the strain is to be a good and successful student. This has the structure of emotional blackmail, and I still twist in discomfort at the restraints.


A month before Kennedy’s assassination, Dallas, right wingers attacked Adalai Stevenson. Dallas earned a reputation as a city of hate.
